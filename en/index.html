The sessions

Guided readings

Each session offers a guided reading of works of contemporary Hispanic literature. Sessions combine reading selected passages aloud, literary analysis and conversation in Spanish.

Each session includes:

  • Reading of the selected texts.
  • Reading selected passages aloud.
  • Literary analysis.
  • Conversation in Spanish.

You can participate in a single session or several, depending on your interests and availability.

The project

About me

I am a teacher of literature and languages and hold a Master's degree in Applied Linguistics.

I was born in Argentina and have taught in New Zealand, the United States, England and now France.

Through Lecturas Hispánicas, I offer a space for reading, literary analysis and conversation in Spanish, where participants can discover contemporary Hispanic literature, deepen their understanding of literary texts and share their own perspectives.
