The Sessions

Guided Readings

Each session offers a guided reading of contemporary Hispanic literary works. Sessions combine the reading aloud of selected passages, literary analysis, and discussion in Spanish.

Each session includes:

  • Reading of the selected texts.
  • Reading aloud of selected passages.
  • Literary analysis.
  • Discussion in Spanish.

The freedom to attend one session or several, according to your interests and availability.

About the Project

About

I am a lecturer in literature and languages and hold a Master's degree in Applied Linguistics.

Born in Argentina, I have taught in New Zealand, the United States, England and now France.

Through Lecturas Hispánicas, I offer a space for reading, literary analysis and discussion in Spanish, where participants can discover contemporary Hispanic literature, deepen their understanding of literary texts and share their own perspectives.
